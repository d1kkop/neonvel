Privacy Policy

Neon Velocity, by Lumio Games · last updated 27 November 2026

Neon Velocity is a racing game you play in your browser. We keep as little about you as we can. This page explains what we store, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Playing without an account

You can play the single-player game without signing in. Your settings, garage choices and progress are then kept only in your own browser (local storage). They never reach our servers.

Signing in with Google

To race online, keep a ranking or buy cars and levels, you sign in with Google. From Google we only receive your Google account ID and your public profile name (scopes openid and profile). We do not receive your e-mail address, contacts or anything else, and we never post anything on your behalf.

What we store for a signed-in player

  • Your Google account ID, so we recognise you the next time you sign in.
  • Your player name in the game. You can change it, and it is visible to other players.
  • Your rating, number of races and wins, your best lap times per level, and online race results.
  • The cars and levels you bought, so they stay unlocked on every device.
  • A session token that keeps you signed in. It expires when you sign out or after a period without use.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. You enter your payment details on Stripe's own checkout page. We never see or store your card or bank details. We only receive a confirmation that the payment succeeded and which item it was for. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Where your data lives

The game and its servers run on Cloudflare. Data is kept only as long as your account exists. We do not sell your data, we do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not share your data with anyone except the services named on this page, which we need to run the game.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the data we keep about you. Deleting it also removes your ranking and purchases. E-mail contact@lumiogames.nl and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Children

Neon Velocity is not aimed at children under 13. If you are under 16, ask a parent or guardian before you sign in or buy anything.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
